# Privacy Policy of Poradniki Publisher

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Poradniki Publisher is a personal, single-user tool that its owner runs on his own computer to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

## Data the tool accesses

With the owner's consent, the tool uses YouTube API Services to access the videos, video metadata, comments and analytics of the YouTube channels managed by the owner's Google account.

## How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage videos on these channels, to read their statistics and to reply to comments on them.

## Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Channel statistics and public comments may be processed by an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) that the owner uses to operate his channels. The data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

## Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Local data is deleted by removing the token files from the owner's computer.

## YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
